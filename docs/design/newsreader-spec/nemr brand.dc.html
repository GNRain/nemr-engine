Something leaves one machine intact and arrives at another

That sentence is the brand. The mark, the motion and the copy all say it, and nothing on the site says anything else. Three mark candidates below — pick one and the rest of the identity follows from it.

Marks

Each drawn from squares and hairlines only, so it survives one colour, 16 px, and a terminal. Shown at 120 px, 32 px and 16 px on the ground it will actually sit on.

A The prompt

A container holding a live prompt. Most legible of the three at 16 px, most terminal-native, least about mist. ASCII form is a single character.

B The dissolve

A solid block going to mist — the breed's name and the product's behaviour in one shape. The most conceptual, and it holds at 16 px because the steps are only three.

C Here and there

Two machines holding one session, the green marking which one is live. Reads fastest as a product idea, but it is the busiest at favicon size.

Motion signature

One movement, used everywhere: the carry. A session goes to mist on one machine, travels as mist, and re-forms whole on another. It is the hero, it is the push in the product, it is the favicon animation, and it is the only ornament the brand owns.
