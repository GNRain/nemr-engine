About

nemr is a container engine for AI coding sessions. It started because the same problem kept happening: a long, productive session with an agent on one machine, and nothing to do about it when the work had to move.

It is built in Rust on containerd and runc, rootless, with no Docker in the stack. The engine is MIT licensed. The cloud storage and sync layer is the commercial side, and it is what pays for the engine to keep being free.

It is a small project. There is no team page because there is not yet a team.

The name

Nemr was my cat. He was a Nebelung — a blue-grey breed whose name comes from Nebel, the German for mist, for the way the silver tips of the coat sit over the grey.

He died. The project is named after him, and the two colours it is built from are his coat and his eyes. That is the whole of it — there is no metaphor being stretched here, and nothing about the software is a tribute.

The mark going from solid to mist is the one place it shows, and it is there because it is also what the product does.
